"How much does a website cost?" is one of the most common questions we get — and one of the hardest to answer without knowing more. A website can cost INR 20,000 or INR 2,000,000 depending on what you actually need.

Here's a transparent breakdown of what determines the cost and what you should realistically budget.

## Why Prices Vary So Much

Website pricing in India varies wildly because "website" means completely different things:

- A freelancer's template website = INR 20,000–50,000
- A professional business website = INR 80,000–250,000
- An e-commerce platform = INR 200,000–600,000
- A custom web application = INR 400,000–2,000,000+

The differences aren't arbitrary — they reflect fundamentally different products.

## The Real Cost Drivers

### 1. Custom Design vs. Template

**Template website:** A designer takes an existing template (WordPress theme, pre-built design) and modifies it with your logo and content. Fast and inexpensive. Looks like hundreds of other websites.

**Custom design:** Designed from scratch to match your brand, your audience, and your business goals. Takes 3–5× longer but produces a website that actually differentiates you.

For a business where your website is a lead generation tool, custom design pays for itself.

### 2. Features and Functionality

Basic features (contact form, about page, service listing) are inexpensive. Each additional feature adds cost:

| Feature | Complexity |
|---|---|
| Blog / CMS | Low |
| WhatsApp chat integration | Low |
| Inquiry forms with email alerts | Low |
| Property listing with search | Medium |
| Online booking system | Medium |
| E-commerce (products, cart, checkout) | High |
| Customer portal / login area | High |
| ERP / inventory integration | Very High |

### 3. Content Creation

Many businesses underestimate the content work:
- Who writes the page copy?
- Who provides professional photos?
- Who creates the service descriptions?

If your developer is also writing your content, expect generic output. Good content is either something you provide or something you pay a copywriter for.

### 4. SEO and Performance Optimization

A fast, SEO-optimized website costs more to build but generates more business. Shortcuts here mean:
- Google ranks you lower
- Mobile visitors bounce because pages load slowly
- You pay for ads to compensate for organic traffic you should have had

### 5. Ongoing Costs

The build cost is just the start. Budget for:
- Hosting: INR 5,000–20,000/year
- Domain: INR 2,000–5,000/year
- Maintenance: INR 10,000–30,000/month (optional but recommended)
- Content updates: as needed

## Realistic Budget Ranges for Indiai SMBs

### INR 20,000–50,000 (Template + Basic)

What you get:
- Template-based design
- 4–6 pages
- Basic contact form
- Mobile responsive (templates usually are)

What you won't get:
- Unique design
- Custom features
- SEO optimization
- Quality copywriting

**Best for:** Very early-stage businesses that just need a web presence.

### INR 80,000–180,000 (Professional Business Website)

What you get:
- Custom or semi-custom design
- 8–12 pages
- WhatsApp integration
- Blog/CMS
- Basic SEO setup
- Google Analytics

**Best for:** Established SMBs using their website as a lead generation tool.

### INR 200,000–500,000 (E-commerce or Advanced)

What you get:
- Full e-commerce capability
- Payment gateway integration
- Inventory management connection
- Customer accounts
- Advanced SEO

**Best for:** Retail businesses selling online or service businesses with complex booking needs.

## Red Flags to Watch For

**Extremely low prices with big promises.** INR 15,000 for a "complete professional website" means a copied template with your logo pasted in.

**No discovery process.** A good website developer asks about your business, your customers, your goals. If they quote without asking any questions, they're building a generic product.

**No maintenance conversation.** A website needs ongoing care. If the developer doesn't mention post-launch support, you'll be calling them as a favor when something breaks.

**Vague deliverables.** Get a specific list: how many pages, which features, timeline, who provides content.
