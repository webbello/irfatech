Every small business reaches a point where leads start slipping through the cracks. The notebook isn't enough. The WhatsApp history is a mess. The spreadsheet never gets updated.

That's when you need a CRM. But which one? Here's an honest guide.

## What a CRM Actually Does for Small Businesses

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system is the single source of truth for all your customer interactions:

- Every lead and inquiry is logged automatically
- Every follow-up is scheduled and tracked
- Every customer's full history is one click away
- Your sales team knows exactly what to do next

When a customer calls and says "we spoke last month," you don't have to panic-search your WhatsApp history.

## The 4 Types of CRM Solutions

### 1. Generic SaaS CRMs (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce)

**Good for:** Businesses with standard sales processes, international companies, teams who can self-onboard.

**Problems for Indiai SMBs:**
- Priced in USD — expensive at current exchange rates
- Designed for Western market workflows
- No WhatsApp integration out of the box
- You adapt your process to the software, not the other way around

### 2. Odoo CRM (with customization)

**Good for:** Businesses that also need ERP, inventory, or billing. Odoo's CRM module is powerful and connects natively to the rest of Odoo.

**Best when:** You're getting a full Odoo ERP — the CRM comes with it and adds no extra cost.

### 3. WhatsApp-Integrated CRM

**Good for:** Indiai SMBs where most business happens over WhatsApp.

This type of CRM is built around WhatsApp as the primary communication channel:
- New WhatsApp inquiries automatically create leads
- Sales reps respond from WhatsApp but it's all logged in the CRM
- Automated follow-up messages sent via WhatsApp

This is what most Indiai retail, real estate, and service businesses actually need.

### 4. Custom-Built CRM

**Good for:** Businesses with unique pipelines that no standard CRM handles well.

Examples:
- A real estate agency with a multi-stage property viewing and offer process
- A clinic tracking patient referrals across locations
- A distributor managing dealer relationships with credit limits and territory management

Custom CRMs cost more upfront but pay back faster because your team actually uses them.

## What to Look for in a CRM for Indiai SMBs

1. **WhatsApp Integration** — Non-negotiable. If your CRM doesn't work with WhatsApp, you'll maintain two systems.
2. **Mobile-friendly** — Your sales team is on the road. Desktop-only CRMs get ignored.
3. **PKR pricing or affordable USD** — Check total annual cost including per-user fees.
4. **Local support** — Who do you call when it breaks at 6pm before a big client meeting?
5. **Custom fields** — Your business has data points that generic CRMs don't anticipate.
