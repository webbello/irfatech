## How it works

The platform runs the same workflow regardless of what's being collected — an inspection, a census, a beneficiary survey:

1. **Assign work** — a supervisor assigns forms and locations to field staff from a central dashboard.
2. **Collect offline** — field staff fill out forms on a phone or tablet, with no connection required.
3. **Capture evidence** — GPS coordinates, photos, QR/barcode scans, and signatures are attached directly to each submission.
4. **Sync to the cloud** — the moment a connection is available, submissions upload automatically in the background.
5. **Route for approval** — each submission moves through a configurable approval workflow to the right reviewer.
6. **Dashboard & analytics** — approved data feeds live dashboards and reports, without a manual re-entry step anywhere in the chain.

This is the same reusable platform behind [mobile inspections](/solutions/mobile-inspection), [farmer registration](/solutions/farmer-registration), [citizen registration](/solutions/citizen-registration), and [beneficiary management](/solutions/beneficiary-management) — the forms, workflow, and terminology change per use case; the underlying system doesn't need rebuilding each time.
