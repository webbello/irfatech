A lot of business websites exist because someone said the business needed one, not because anyone expects it to actually bring in customers. That's a missed opportunity — a website is one of the few channels that works while you're asleep.

## Why most SMB websites don't generate leads

Three problems show up over and over:

- **There's no clear next step.** A visitor reads the page and there's nothing obvious to click — no clear way to enquire, book, or ask a question.
- **It doesn't say what makes you different.** Generic copy that could describe any competitor doesn't give anyone a reason to choose you specifically.
- **It's slow, or it's not built to be found.** A site that takes six seconds to load, or that search engines struggle to read cleanly, loses visitors before they see anything at all.

None of these are expensive problems to fix. They're mostly about intent — building the site to do a specific job, rather than to exist.

## What we build instead

Every website we build starts from one question: what should a visitor do here, and how do we make that as easy as possible? In practice that means:

- **A clear path to contact** — whether that's a form, a WhatsApp button, or both, visible without hunting for it.
- **Content that's actually about your business** — your services, your process, and what makes working with you different, not generic industry filler.
- **Speed as a feature, not an afterthought** — fast load times on a phone with an average connection, not just on a developer's laptop on fibre.
- **Built to be found by Google and by AI assistants** — structured so both can read what your business actually does and recommend it accurately.

## Connected to everything else

A lead-generation website is most valuable when it's not an island. A contact form that creates a CRM entry automatically, WhatsApp integration that starts a conversation the moment someone clicks, and analytics that tell you which pages actually bring in enquiries — that's the difference between a website that supports the rest of your systems and one that sits disconnected from them, quietly out of date.
