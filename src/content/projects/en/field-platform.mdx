## The Brief

Government agencies, NGOs, and field teams across our markets were running inspections, surveys, and registrations on paper, or on consumer survey tools that assume a connection a rural field visit rarely has. Every new use case meant building — or re-building — the same offline data-capture problem from scratch.

## What We Built

Field Platform is our reusable, offline-first data-collection platform: one core system — authentication, forms, workflow, dashboard, reports, notifications, documents, API — that different sector solutions configure rather than rebuild.

- Fully offline form-filling: GPS, photos, QR/barcode scans, and signatures captured on-device
- Automatic sync the moment a connection is available, with no manual export step
- Configurable approval workflows that route submissions to the right reviewer
- Live dashboards and reports built from the same field data, not a separate re-entry step

## One Platform, Different Solutions

The same underlying system now powers [mobile inspections](/solutions/mobile-inspection), [school inspections](/solutions/school-inspection), [farmer registration](/solutions/farmer-registration), [citizen registration](/solutions/citizen-registration), and [NGO beneficiary management](/solutions/beneficiary-management). Each solution customises its forms, workflow, and terminology — none of them rebuild the platform underneath.

This is the same philosophy behind [Landflix](/projects/landflix), our real estate platform: build the reusable core once, and let each vertical be a configuration of it rather than a separate project.

## Results

Field Platform lets us take on a new sector — a new inspection type, a new registration programme — as a configuration exercise measured in weeks, not a from-scratch build measured in months.
