## The Brief

Ashaz Autoz runs four businesses under one roof from their yard in Ponte Foun, Comoro, Dili: vehicle sales, vehicle imports from Japan, Singapore, and Australia, rentals, and workshop diagnostics. They needed one site that could carry all four without turning into four disconnected brochures — and one their team could keep updating with new stock themselves.

## What We Built

A dealership platform built on the same stack we run our own sites on:

- A browsable, filterable vehicle inventory — available stock and upcoming arrivals, each with pricing, mileage, and specs
- Dedicated sections for every line of business: sales, imports, rentals, and the diagnostics workshop
- Enquiry and contact flows tuned for how Timor-Leste customers actually reach out, including direct WhatsApp click-to-chat
- Installable as a Progressive Web App, so returning customers can open it like a native app
- A blog and social feed integration to keep the site current between vehicle drops

## Results

Ashaz Autoz is a live, active business site today — 500+ vehicles sold and 1,000+ customers served through it, by their own published numbers. It's also a proof point for the same discipline we bring to every client build: one connected system instead of a brochure site plus a separate spreadsheet plus a WhatsApp number nobody's tracking.
