## The Brief

Real estate technology in the markets we serve tends to be either expensive, enterprise-grade software built for large developers, or a patchwork of spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper listings held together by habit. There wasn't a real estate platform sized for the businesses actually operating day to day in this space — so we built one.

## What We Built

Landflix is IRFAtech's own SaaS platform for digital property management and real estate technology: property listings, management workflows, and the day-to-day tools a small real estate operation needs to stay organised, without requiring a dedicated IT department to run it.

- Digital property listings and portfolio management
- Workflow tools for the people running the day-to-day operation
- Built on the same stack we use for client ERP and custom software work — Odoo, Laravel, and Vue.js
- Support and monitoring under the same SLA discipline we apply to every client system

## Why We Built It Ourselves

Landflix is both a product in its own right and a proof point. It's the clearest evidence we can offer that our stack builds things meant to last, not just meant to launch — because we run it, support it, and depend on it exactly the way our clients depend on the systems we build for them.

## Results

Landflix runs as a live product today, and the discipline of operating it — support commitments, scope control, choosing a stack for the long term rather than the newest option — now shapes how we scope every client engagement.
