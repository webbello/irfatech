_Last updated: 30 August 2026_

IRFATECH ("we", "us", "our") operates [irfatech.in](https://irfatech.in) and provides
software development, ERP, CRM, automation, and related services. This policy
explains what personal information we handle and why.

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us.** When you submit the contact form, start a WhatsApp
  enquiry, subscribe to the newsletter, or email us, we receive your name, contact
  details, and whatever you choose to tell us about your project.
- **Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our host and
  analytics record standard technical data — IP address, browser type, pages
  visited, and referring URL — to keep the site secure and understand how it is
  used.
- **Cookies.** The site uses only the cookies required for it to function and,
  where enabled, privacy-friendly analytics. We do not use advertising cookies.

## How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver and support the services
you engage us for, send you the newsletter if you asked for it, meet legal and
accounting obligations, and improve the website.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers
who help us operate (for example, email delivery, hosting, and analytics
providers), and where required by law.

## Retention

We keep enquiry and client records for as long as needed to provide our services
and to meet legal, tax, and contractual requirements, then delete or anonymise
them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold
about you, or to stop contacting you. Email
[irfatech.in@gmail.com](mailto:irfatech.in@gmail.com) and we will respond within a
reasonable period.
