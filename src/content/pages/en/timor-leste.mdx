IRFATECH is a software development company serving **Timor-Leste** — businesses in
Dili, Baucau, and the districts, along with government agencies, NGOs, and
international organisations working in the country. We build websites, mobile
apps, ERP and CRM systems, WhatsApp automation, and offline-first field data
tools, and we work in **Tetun, Portuguese, and English**.

We are also active in India, where the company was founded in 2009. The same team
and the same stack serve both markets.

## What we build

- **[Business websites](/services/websites/)** — fast, credible sites with
  WhatsApp built in, so an enquiry becomes a conversation immediately.
- **[Mobile apps](/services/mobile-apps/)** — iOS and Android from one codebase,
  for customers, field teams, or internal operations.
- **[ERP for small business](/solutions/erp-for-small-business/)** — Odoo-based
  inventory, billing, and accounting in one connected system.
- **[CRM and sales automation](/services/crm-automation/)** — track every lead
  and follow up on a schedule, not on memory.
- **[WhatsApp automation](/services/whatsapp-automation/)** — order updates,
  reminders, and instant replies over the WhatsApp Business API.
- **[Custom software](/services/custom-software/)** — dashboards, portals, and
  business applications built around how you actually work.
- **[Digital field data collection](/solutions/digital-field-data-collection/)**
  — offline-first surveys, inspections, and registrations for government and NGO
  programmes, syncing when a connection is available.

## Who we work with in Timor-Leste

Small and growing businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups;
government departments running inspections, registrations, and surveys; and NGOs
that need to register beneficiaries and report to donors from the field.

One example: **[Ashaz Autoz](/projects/ashaz-autoz/)** in Ponte Foun, Comoro,
Dili — a full dealership platform covering vehicle sales, imports, rentals, and
workshop services on one site, built on the same stack we use for our own
products.

## How we work

We are a **service-area business** — we do not run a walk-in office in Dili.
Projects are scoped over a call, delivered in short cycles you can follow on a
staging link, and priced as one fixed number rather than billed by the hour.
Support is part of every proposal, so there is no gap between launch and someone
answering the phone.
